# Code of Conduct

The Flocord community is welcoming to everyone, regardless of their background or characteristics.

We expect everyone contributing to Flocord to treat others with respect and help maintain an open, friendly, and inclusive community.

**DO:**

* Have empathy and be respectful toward others.
* Respect different opinions and perspectives, even when you disagree.
* Give and accept constructive criticism.
* Help others and contribute positively to the community.

**DON'T:**

* Use offensive, hateful, or derogatory language.
* Troll, spam, or intentionally disrupt discussions.
* Personally attack, harass, or target other community members.

Repeated violations of these guidelines may result in restricted access to the Flocord repository or community spaces.

If you believe someone is violating these guidelines or you feel you have been treated unfairly, please avoid retaliation and report the issue to the moderation team.

For support, discussions, or community help, please use the official Flocord community channels:
https://github.com/Code-Flocord/Flocord
